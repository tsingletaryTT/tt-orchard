## The token 6 divergence of the first chat prompt (explained)

The served greedy run of the first chat prompt left the reference at token 6 while the teacher-forced port agreed with the reference there. A first look at the diag record showed a reference gap
of 12.3 logits at that position, which would have been a serving fault. It was a fault in `analyze_divergence.py`: it keyed the three records by the first 40 characters of the prompt, the two chat
prompts share those characters, and the first chat prompt's row used the second one's logits. With the records matched by position (and the prompt text checked), all four free-run
divergences are near ties: the reference's gap between its top two is 0.125 logits, and the port's top two are the same two tokens, two of them with exactly equal logits (21.375 and 21.375
for ' and' and ' in' at token 6; 16.625 and 16.625 for '1' and '50' at token 18) and two ranked the other way round (gap 0.125). The served run is deterministic: on a fresh server the prompt left at
token 6 on both repeats and again after a long prompt in between (`served-diag.json`), and the other prompts left at the same indexes (18 and 27) every time. So the difference is not state left by earlier requests.
Not tested: that `argmax` and `topk` break the exact tie differently (the served run took the second of the two equal tokens in the teacher-forced listing).

## Bugs found on the way

- The first two server starts on the chips never became ready. The cause was not the mesh open (every bundle environment variant opened in 1.7 to 9 s; `2026-11-22-aion-mesh-bisect.md`) but
  `ttnn.from_torch` of a memory-mapped checkpoint tensor with the PyPI ttnn 0.80.0 wheel (issue 813). The packaged port copies each tensor out of the mapping; this is its only difference from the stage 3 port.
- `config/can.aion.toml` named the weights `allenai/...` after a search and replace from the Olmo spec; `run.sh` would have downloaded from the wrong organisation. Fixed before any boot.
- `install.sh` of a ttnn 0.80.0 thin bundle stops on a host with SFPI older than 7.85.0. The card's Prerequisites say so.
- `analyze_divergence.py` mixed up prompts that share their first 40 characters (above). The Olmo copy of the script has the same key.
- The first benchmark chose a filler prompt whose token count (9,101 and 8,703) passed the server's 8,192 limit, and a decode-at-length prompt that ended after one token. Both were redone (`bench-long*.json`).
- `gozer run --fresh` and `gozer acquire --fresh` stayed queued with all four chips free after the reboot; without `--fresh` the lease was granted at once.

## Decision

Not published. A3 fails by the letter (4 of 5; 5 of 5 only after a normalisation the pre-written check did not have). The other bars pass. Two points the operator should weigh before waiving A3:
the chat-route decode rate is about half of the P1 figure (pending a quiet re-run), and the port reruns the whole sequence for every token, so decode with a 7.5k-token context is 0.093 tok/s.
